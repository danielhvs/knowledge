**Move back** a meeting means postpone it, push it later. It does not mean earlier. "Move up" or "pull in" means earlier.

Português: "Dá para adiar a revisão dos testes de aceitação em 90 minutos?"

Example:
- "Can we move the acceptance testing walk through back 90m?" = push the meeting 90 minutes later.

Pieces:
1. **move back / push back / push out**: later.
2. **move up / pull in / bring forward**: earlier.
3. **90m**: 90 minutes. Written shorthand in chat: `30m`, `1h`, `2d`.
4. **acceptance testing**: checks that the software does what the users or client need, usually before release.
5. **walk through** (or walkthrough): a meeting where people go through something step by step, here reviewing the tests together.
