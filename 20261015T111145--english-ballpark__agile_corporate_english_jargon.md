In Jira and agile meetings, a **ballpark** is a rough, non-committal estimate given quickly, before anyone has done the detailed analysis. It answers "roughly how big is this?" and is usually good to within about a factor of two.

Examples:
- "Ballpark it: is this a day or a sprint?"
- "Ballpark is 3 to 5 story points, but we need to look at the auth code first."
- "Can you give me a ballpark for the migration?" means "a number I can put on a roadmap, not a commitment."

It's useful for:
- **Triage**: deciding whether a ticket is worth refining now.
- **Roadmapping**: rough timelines for things that aren't scoped yet.
- **Comparing options**: "option A is a ballpark of 2 weeks, option B is 2 months."

People often hedge it with "ballpark" so nobody treats it as a firm estimate. If you're asked for a ballpark, give a range and say what you're assuming. A firm estimate comes later, after refinement.
