The sentence means the dashboard shows the place and time of a payment, but the verb "lit" is unusual in this context.

"Lit" is the past tense of "light," as in a fire that was lit. Slang uses "lit" to mean exciting, and "lit up" means illuminated. None of these fits a payment, so the author probably meant "logged," "recorded," or "triggered."

Example sentences:
- The dashboard lists where and when each payment was logged.
- The report shows the location and timestamp of every transaction.
- Each payment recorded on the dashboard includes the store and the date.

When to use: Use "logged" or "recorded" for a payment that was saved in a system. Use "where and when" to name the location and time together. Avoid "lit" for payments unless you are writing casual slang on purpose.
