slang english nostalgia phrase

That's OG November is an informal, slangy English phrase that praises something as the original, classic, old-school version of November, the way it used to be or the way it should be.

Example sentences:
- "Staying in with hot chocolate and old movies? That's OG November."
- "Everyone's wearing their old college hoodies again. Pure OG November."
- "No sunscreen, no plans, just leaves and rain. That's OG November."

Use it casually with friends or in social posts when you want to celebrate a nostalgic, authentic feel of the season. Avoid it in formal writing, where the slang and vague reference would confuse readers.
