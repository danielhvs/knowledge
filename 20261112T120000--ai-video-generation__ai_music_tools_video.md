How a full music video (song of almost 5 min) was made in one day with free AI tools.

Pipeline:
1. Music: [Suno](https://suno.com). Already used it in another project, so downloads work there. Plan: migrate to [Google Flow Music](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6760315723) (Google's music app, Lyria model, part of the Flow suite).
2. Images: [Google Flow](https://labs.google/flow). Image generation looks unlimited so far.
3. Animate the images into video clips, also in Google Flow (Veo model). The free daily credits give about 30s of video per day. Two Google accounts = about 1 min/day.
4. Edit everything together.

Limits hit:
1. A ~5 min song needs ~5 min of video, but free credits give ~1 min/day. Result was a "Frankenstein": still images mixed with video, many repeated scenes.
2. Other free platforms work but are annoying: some add a watermark, which then has to be removed in the edit.

Lesson: with free credits, either pick a short song or spread the video generation over several days.
