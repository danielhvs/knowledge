# euro-park:

Theme park with a bunch of "places": each for a given country

1. If possible, avoid weekends and summer school holidays.
2. Europa-Park allows you to bring your own food and drinks.
3. Use the Free "VirtualLine": Download the official Europa-Park App before you arrive.
4. Arrive Before Opening: Aim to be at the turnstiles by 8:15 AM. The park often opens its gates early, allowing you to walk into the front area and ride Voletarium (a popular flying theater) before the rest of the park officially opens at 9:00 AM.
5. There is a waterpark nearby: [rulantica](denote:20260912T115710)

Address:
1. Europa-Park is located in Rust, Baden-Württemberg, Germany, situated at the edge of the Black Forest near the borders with France and Switzerland.
2. Its specific address is: Europa-Park-Straße 2, 77977 Rust, Germany
